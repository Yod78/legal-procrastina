Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Procrastina is a personal, single-user task-tracking application. It is developed and operated solely by its author for their own private use, and is not made available to the public.

Procrastina connects to the author's own Google Calendar and Google Drive accounts, using Google's OAuth 2.0 protocol, exclusively to:

  • Read the author's Google Calendar events, to display them alongside planned tasks
  • Create, update, and delete Google Calendar events representing the author's own planned work
  • Store periodic backups of the application's own data in the author's Google Drive

No data accessed through these integrations is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party. No data is used for advertising or analytics purposes. All data stays strictly between the author's own accounts and the author's own private server.

Since this application has a single user who is also its developer, there is no mechanism for other individuals' data to be collected or processed.
